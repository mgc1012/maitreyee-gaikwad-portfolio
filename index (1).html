How I approach a product problem

My strongest work is not just the final screen. It is the reasoning between the user problem and the outcome.

01 · Frame

Understand the user and the system

Start with customer research, interviews, market signals, and the operational context around the problem.

02 · Decide

Turn insight into product direction

Define the audience, prioritize the highest-value needs, shape requirements, and connect the product to a measurable goal.

03 · Learn

Build, test, and improve

Use prototypes, feedback, analytics, and cross-functional collaboration to improve the product after launch.
